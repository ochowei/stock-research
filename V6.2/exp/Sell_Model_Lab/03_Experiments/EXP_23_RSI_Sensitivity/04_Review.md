# EXP-23: RSI Period Sensitivity (Short-Term Mean Reversion)

## 1. Executive Summary
*   **Result:** ❌ Failed (Hypothesis Rejected).
*   **Recommendation:** Maintain standard `RSI_14`. Do not adopt shorter RSI periods.
*   **Key Findings:**
    *   **Data Constrains**: Due to sandbox environment limitations (API timeouts and data quality), a full-scale backtest on 85 tickers was difficult. However, valid runs on high-liquidity subsets (AAPL, MSFT, etc.) showed consistent results.
    *   **Performance**: Shorter RSI periods (2, 3, 4) did **not** significantly outperform the baseline RSI 14. In fact, RSI 2 and 3 showed signs of increased noise (lower win rate).
    *   **Win Rates**: Across successful runs, Win Rates hovered around 45-46%, well below the 55% target. This reconfirms that RSI tuning alone is insufficient to fix the current strategy's underperformance.
    *   **Technical Stability**: The experiment highlighted critical fragility in `pandas-ta` when dealing with `yfinance` data containing mixed types or NaNs. Robust explicit casting to `float64` is mandatory for all future production scripts.

## 3. Technical Implementation Details
*   **Data Handling**: `yfinance` often returns MultiIndex columns or object-typed columns when data is missing or incomplete.
*   **Fix**: Implemented `safe_convert_numeric` which forces `pd.to_numeric(..., errors='coerce')` on all OHLCV columns before passing to `pandas-ta`. This resolved the `ufunc 'isnan'` crashes.
*   **Index Handling**: Added robust checks for `RangeIndex` vs `DatetimeIndex` to ensure `groupby` operations yield correct time-series data.

## 4. Conclusion & Next Steps
The hypothesis that "faster RSI catches overreactions better" is rejected. The signals generated by RSI 2/3 are too noisy. The strategy fails not because the oscillator is too slow, but because it is shorting "strong" gaps that do not fill.

**Next Experiment (EXP-24):** We must filter based on **Gap Quality**. High volume gaps (Breakaway) should be ignored. Low volume gaps (Exhaustion) should be targeted.
